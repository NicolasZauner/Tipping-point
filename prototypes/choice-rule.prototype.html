Does the choice rule behave like a car market?

Every buyer has a tolerance: how much more, as a TCO ratio against a combustion car, they'd pay for the newest powertrain. Each of the five groups has its tolerances spread around a mean. A buyer picks the powertrain with the best score, and the score combines the tolerance, the TCO ratio and two extras that calibration will fit. Press the buttons and watch who switches. The numbers are illustrative: Germany 2026, medium class, a private buyer with a home charger.
